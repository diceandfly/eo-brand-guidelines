    Applications

    Approach

    This page is a set of reference drafts that apply the EO graphic system to various media. Refer to the look and feel, layout composition and media-specific direction of the applied graphic pattern, but actual production may differ by medium and specification, so give priority to the rules in each chapter and proceed with the approval of the EO design team.

    When placing the graphic pattern on a page, we recommend following these rules.

    • Match the pattern’s density to viewing distance. On large pages seen from afar, such as billboards, push complexity all the way up to hold the eye through density; keep posters seen up close at medium complexity, and business cards held in the hand simpler still.
    • Lead the eye with the direction of the threads. Arrange the threads so that their compression gathers in one place, leading the eye to the mark or message there.
    • Rebuild the pattern to fit the format. Every graphic pattern is a responsive system that can be rearranged to the proportion of its rectangular area, but when stretching or shrinking the same pattern, check that the threads in the compression section have not become too dense.

    Digital

    Digital touchpoints such as the web · Instagram feed · SNS profile · YouTube channel. The grid system in this guideline is based mainly on static pages, so web and digital content, whose screen sizes vary, should give priority to a responsive layout suited to each environment.

    Print

    Printed media such as business cards · posters · billboards · hanging banners.

    • Color combinations follow Graphic Pattern › Color: prefer Solid Gray · Space Black backgrounds, and use a Builder Neon background sparingly to assert the brand’s presence strongly.
    • Print with the CMYK values in EO Palette. Screen RGB · HEX values shift in print, so build print files in CMYK mode from the start. Where color fidelity matters, or a Builder Neon background covers a large area, print it as a Pantone spot color.
    • Air White can be replaced by white paper instead of ink. The same values look different on different papers and materials, so check the colors with a proof before the final run.
    • On small pages whose front and back work together, such as business cards, put the graphic pattern on one side and the information on the other, and base the margins and grid on the side with the main content (Zoning).

    Outdoor media such as billboards · hanging banners, read from afar and in a short time, give the graphic pattern area generous room as in the examples in Graphic Pattern › Zoning, and reduce the message to about one to three lines of headline. Decide the pattern density by weighing both visual impact and viewing distance, and set the symbol size by the ratio to viewing distance in Symbol › Scalability. On vertical media that stand on the ground or hang from a pole, such as hanging banners, the graphic pattern area can sit at the bottom.

    Merch

    A brand merch line of apparel such as tees · hoodies · caps, plus hang tags · stickers · pin badges. On the tee, place a small EO symbol on the front chest and a large dedicated graphic of simplified units on the back; on the hoodie, place a pattern that blends a pair at an equal ratio (Dimension) on the front.

    • For apparel graphics, screen printing is recommended so that vivid single colors stand out. Mix the inks to the Pantone values in EO Palette. Screen printing struggles with very thin lines and gradients, so instead of dense patterns whose threads get too thin, use graphics of simplified units.
    • Treat the fabric color as the graphic’s background color and follow the combinations in Graphic Pattern › Color. When printing light ink on dark fabric, lay down a white underbase first for full color.
    • Always keep the symbol on apparel small and compact, and prefer heat-transfer printing, which carries the crisp graphic as it is. This is because embroidery brings out a strongly analog mood.
    • For merch rendered in the color of its material, such as metal · engraving, apply the form alone instead of a color combination, and always render it in a single color.
    • For paper accessories such as hang tags · stickers, print colors follow the standards in Print.
